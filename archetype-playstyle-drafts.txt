ARCHETYPE PLAYSTYLE DRAFTS - FOR REVIEW
========================================
Not yet added to content/archetypes/_index.json - drafted here for you to review,
edit, and paste in yourself.

Written in the same voice/format as your existing playstyles: combat-only,
**bold** for the specific Talent/Spell being called out, no mention of any
Narrative Talent. Each one is grounded in that archetype's actual prepared
Combat Talents, spells, and equipment (cross-checked against the rules text in
content/talents/combat.json and content/spells/*.json) - not just vibes.

Where an archetype holds a Combat Talent whose bonus doesn't cleanly click with
the rest of its kit (e.g. a talent that wants no armor on a character who wears
armor), I either left it out or phrased it as a situational fallback rather than
overstating a synergy that isn't really there - flagged inline with [note] where
that happened, so you can decide whether to swap gear/talents instead of the text.


SWORD
=====

Assassin
--------
Open a fight from range by throwing your Throwing Knife - a successful hit with
**Thrown Weapons Adept** hands you a bonus momentum immediately. Close in and
strike with your shortsword or dagger; **Sword Adept** and **Dual Wielding**
both shave down the cost to Swift Task into a second attack with whichever
blade is free, letting you chain hits before the enemy can react.

Once you've landed a hit, use **Stealthy Fighter** to Hide as a Minor Action -
vanish back into the shadows to reposition, line up your next throw, or avoid
being ganged up on between turns.


AXE
===

Death Knight
------------
Make your battleaxe your **Spellblade** weapon, so you can swing it using
Presence instead of Brawn - and cast Dark spells right through it, no Spell
Implement needed. Before charging in, cast **Siphoning Strike** to imbue your
axe: the extra damage lands alongside your **Axe Adept** rerolls, and you heal
for every Effect you roll.

When an enemy manages to wound you, retaliate on your next turn - **Vengeful**
grants a hefty 2 bonus momentum against anyone who damaged you this round,
fueling repeat swings or a follow-up **Soul Drain** to seep away more of their
life for your own.

Guardian
--------
Take the Guard Major Action to protect an ally - wearing armor nets you a
bonus momentum from **Defensive Wall** for doing so, and **Close Protection**
lets that ally move freely at your side without leaving your protection,
keeping them shielded as the fight shifts.

Wield your round shield to Block incoming attacks: **Shield Master** doubles
the temporary HP you gain when an Effect is rolled. Once per session,
designate an ally with **Willing Shield** so you can throw yourself between
them and an attacker's blow, assisting their defense with your own weapon's
Attribute and Skirmish.

Warrior
-------
Thanks to **Heavy Lifting**, wield your greataxe in one hand without penalty
so you can also carry your shield - the best of both offense and defense. Go
berserk as a Minor Action with **Berserker**: you gain a bonus die and +2[CD]
on every melee swing, and **Axe Adept** lets you reroll any damage dice that
come up low.

Before the real fighting starts, **Antagonist** makes your Demoralize hit
twice as hard, softening an enemy's resolve before you close the distance.
Remember that while berserk you can't recover HP or Willpower and enemies
gain a bonus die against you too - so pick your moment to rage.

Reaver
------
**Heavy Lifting** lets you wield your greataxe in a single hand, freeing your
other hand for a handaxe - true dual-wielding of two Axe-tag weapons. Attack
with one, then use **Dual Wielding**'s reduced Swift Task cost to follow up
with the other; **Axe Adept** lets you reroll any lackluster damage dice from
either strike.

When you Shove a foe out of position, **Juggernaut** sends them flying 3
spaces instead of 1 - useful for putting distance between you and a target
you'd rather isolate, or knocking someone off a ledge or into a hazard.


SPEAR
=====

Paladin
-------
Make your glaive your **Spellblade** weapon so you can strike with Faith
instead of Coordination, and cast Light spells through it freely. Before
wading in, cast **Holy Smite** to imbue the blade with radiant power, then
thrust with **Spear Adept**'s bonus die backing up the swing.

At the start of a rough turn, take a **Defensive Stance** for 5 temporary HP
to weather the return blow. Between clashes, reach out with **Lay on Hands**
to mend a wounded ally at your side, ward one with **Divine Protection** from
range, or soften a target with **Guiding Ray** so your allies' follow-up
attacks land easier.

Bulwark
-------
Plant yourself at the front with your tower shield - its Concealing quality
gives adjacent allies Half-Cover from ranged attacks, while **Shield Master**
doubles the temporary HP your Block gains from a rolled Effect. Take the
Guard Major Action to watch over an ally: **Defensive Wall** rewards you with
bonus momentum for wearing armor while you do, and **Close Protection** lets
your ward move freely at your side without stepping outside your protection.

When an opening appears, thrust with your spear - **Spear Adept**'s bonus die
keeps your strikes reliable even while your attention is split between
offense and holding the line.


BOW
===

Arcane Archer
-------------
Thanks to **Spellshot**, your Spellblade spells work through your bow instead
of a melee weapon - imbue your shortbow with **Volcanic Strike**, **Frozen
Strike**, or **Thunderous Strike** before firing to layer elemental damage
and a status effect onto your shot. **Bow Adept** lets you choose the better
result when you reroll on the Aim Minor Action, keeping your aimed shots
reliable.

Use **Skip** to reposition - **Warp** extends its range further than most,
letting you put real distance between yourself and anyone closing in. When
forced into melee, **Arcane Parry** lets you defend with Magick instead of
your physical Attributes.


ARCANE MAGICK
=============

Fulgermancer
------------
Strike from range with **Electric Arc**, then use **Fulgermancer** to juice
up your next Lightning spell - Arcing Bolt to catch multiple foes in its
burst, Distant Spark to stretch your range further, or Static Vulnerability
to leave a stunned target wide open for an ally's follow-up. **Armis Arcane**
keeps you surprisingly durable while unarmored and unshielded.

When things get too close, cast **Invisibilis** and immediately Hide as a
free action thanks to **Shadow Cloak** - or **Skip** away entirely, **Warp**
stretching the teleport further than most casters manage. Either way, you're
gone before retaliation ever lands.

Pyromancer
----------
Ride into range on your horse - **Cavalry Adept** removes the usual
Difficulty increase for casting while mounted - and rain down **Firebolt** on
your enemies. Apply **Pyromancer** to your next Fire spell for Spreading
Flames to catch a cluster of foes, Vicious Flames for a straight damage
boost, or Distant Flames to strike from further away.

With your Spell Implement in hand, **Augmentier** turns any Bonus Damage
momentum spend into extra damage dice instead of flat points, letting a
well-placed hit spiral out of control. Use **Skip** to reposition and
**Electric Arc** to punish anyone resistant to fire, and fall back on
**Arcane Parry** to defend with Magick if an enemy closes the distance.

Cryomancer
----------
Stay unarmored and shieldless to get the most from **Counterspell** - a
bonus, rerollable die to defend against enemy spells - while **Armis Arcane**
still grants you 2 Resistance to offset the risk. Freeze foes at range with
**Frigid Grasp**, then apply **Cryomancer** to your next Ice spell: Encasing
Ice to lock a Slowed target down entirely, Frostbite to catch them Off-Guard
instead, or Brittle Ice to expose them to your allies' attacks.

With your Spell Implement in hand, **Augmentier** converts Bonus Damage
momentum spends into extra damage dice. If an enemy gets in your face, imbue
your handaxe with **Frozen Strike** to slow them down, or **Skip** away
before they get the chance.

Spellstalker
------------
Strike from hiding with **Electric Arc** or **Frozen Strike**, then use
**Stealthy Fighter** to Hide again as a Minor Action the moment your attack
connects. Cast **Invisibilis** to vanish outright - **Shadow Cloak** lets you
immediately Hide as a free action the instant you turn invisible, layering
concealment on concealment.

When you need to close distance or bail out entirely, **Skip** away with
**Warp**'s extended range. Your buckler's Effortless quality keeps your
off-hand free without slowing you down, so you're never caught without an
escape route.


LIGHT MAGICK
============

Mendicant
---------
Reach out and heal the wounded with **Lay on Hands**, then apply **Vitalurge**
to your next Healing spell - Soothing Touch for more HP restored, Restorative
Ward to layer on temporary HP, or Cleansing Light to burn off a lingering
condition alongside the healing. **Divine Protection** wards an ally before
they take a hit at all, and **Guiding Ray** both damages a foe and makes them
easier for your allies to hit.

With your Spell Implement in hand, **Augmentier** turns Bonus Damage momentum
into extra damage dice when you do need to fight back. [note: Counterspell's
bonus die needs no armor/shield, but this build wears medium armor - line
below phrases it as a situational fallback rather than a reliable feature]
**Counterspell** gives you a fighting chance to shrug off a return spell if
you're ever caught without your armor.

Luminary
--------
Strike from range with **Guiding Ray**, then apply **Luminurge** to your next
Radiant spell to layer on a status effect - Dazzling Flash to catch them
Off-Guard, Weakening Glare to sap their strength, or Exposing Glint to open
them up for your allies. **Touch of Haste** sends an ally darting into
position or out of danger with a burst of speed.

Mend the wounded with **Lay on Hands**, and with your Spell Implement in
hand, **Augmentier** turns Bonus Damage momentum into extra damage dice on
the rare occasion you need to hit hard. **Fortified Mind** lets you shrug off
attempts to shake your resolve mid-fight.

Inquisitor
----------
Use **Inquisitor's Eye** to sniff out anyone hiding from you, then bear down
on spellcasters especially - **Mage's Bane** lets you punish an enemy the
instant they cast a spell, either with an immediate Opportunity Attack if
they're close or a full-speed charge to close the gap if they're not. Imbue
your shortsword with **Holy Smite** before you strike.

Demoralize is your other weapon: **Antagonist** adds extra damage to a
successful Demoralize, and **Provocateur** lets you tack the Provoking damage
effect onto either your attack or your Demoralize, dragging an enemy's
attention onto you. **Castigate** batters a target's Willpower directly,
softening their resolve from range before you ever close in.

War Priest
----------
Make your warhammer your **Spellblade** weapon, striking with Faith instead
of Brawn and casting Light spells through it without a Spell Implement.
Imbue it with **Holy Smite** before you swing - your warhammer's own
Knockdown effect on top of the extra radiant damage can flatten an enemy
outright.

**Mage's Bane** lets you punish enemy spellcasters the moment they cast, with
an Opportunity Attack if they're close or a full charge if they're not.
Between swings, mend allies with **Lay on Hands**, soften a target from range
with **Guiding Ray** or **Castigate**, and use **Luminurge** to layer a
status effect onto your next Radiant spell.


DARK MAGICK
===========

Shadow Caller
-------------
Strike from range with **Penumbra**, then apply **Noctocraft** to your next
Shadow spell - Grip of Terror to leave a hit target Frightened, Enfeebling
Shroud to set up a devastating follow-up Demoralize, or Last Will to hit even
harder once you've taken a Wound yourself. Imbue your dagger with **Shadow
Blade** or **Geist Strike** before closing in for the kill.

Disappear the instant the fighting turns against you: **Stealthy Fighter**
lets you Hide as a Minor Action right after a successful attack, and casting
**Umbral Shroud** turns you invisible outright - **Shadow Cloak** then lets
you Hide again immediately, keeping you one step ahead of anyone trying to
find you.

Plague Doctor
-------------
Poison a foe's blood with **Blood Sickness**, then apply **Hemocraft** to your
next Blood spell - Open Veins to make them harder to heal, Diseased Blood to
worsen the lingering damage, or Blood Sense to stretch your reach. **Soul
Drain** seeps away an enemy's life force at close range and hands the stolen
HP right back to you.

When an ally goes down, **Combat Medic** gives you a bonus die on First Aid
even in the thick of a fight, and **Blood Heal** lets you trade your own HP
to mend theirs at a steep exchange rate. With your Spell Implement in hand,
**Augmentier** turns Bonus Damage momentum into extra damage dice for when
you need to strike back yourself.

Nosferatu
---------
Seep away life with **Soul Drain**, then apply **Thymocraft** to your next
Soul spell - Weakening Drain to sap a target's strength, Lethargic Drain to
slow them down, or Thirsting Drain to heal for even more when the spell
restores your HP. **Siphoning Strike** turns your handaxe itself into a
life-draining weapon for when enemies close the distance.

**Gremorie** gives your Tome an extra Spell Slot, letting you prepare more of
your draining spells than most casters manage, and **Augmentier** converts
Bonus Damage momentum into extra damage dice with your Spell Implement in
hand.

Night Rider
-----------
Charge in mounted, lance leveled - its Saddleback quality means you don't
suffer the usual Difficulty increase for attacking on horseback, and **Spear
Adept** adds a reliable bonus die to the strike. Make your lance your
**Spellblade** weapon so you can attack with Presence and cast Dark spells
through it without a Spell Implement.

Before the charge, cast **Penumbra** or imbue your lance with **Shadow
Blade** for extra bite, and apply **Noctocraft** to your next Shadow spell to
leave a hit target Frightened. **Blood Bargain** lets you trade HP for a
burst of Willpower (or an easier Magick task) when you need the extra push,
and **Siphoning Strike** keeps you topped up in a drawn-out melee.


AUTHORITY
=========

Bishop
------
Ride at the head of your allies - **Cavalry Adept** removes the usual
Difficulty increase for acting from horseback - and take the **Direct**
Major Action to let an ally act on your turn. **Commander** grants a bonus
momentum every time you Direct or Issue Orders, and **Bannerlord** lets you
wield your banner without tying up a hand, still granting a reroll on
Authority Tasks or Direct itself.

Support from range with **Guiding Ray** to soften a foe and set up an ally's
attack, mend wounds with **Lay on Hands**, and use **Prayer of Fortune** to
hand an ally an Advantage right when they need it most.

Strategist
----------
Set the battlefield before the fight even starts - **Trapper** lets you lay
hidden Spring, Steel, or Net traps to knock back, immobilize, or ensnare
anyone who stumbles into them. Open with **Pinpoint** to size up a target,
and **Tactician** hands you 2 bonus momentum for it, on top of unlocking the
**Direct** Major Action.

**Commander** adds another bonus momentum every time you Direct or Issue
Orders, letting your allies act more often than they could alone, and
**Helping Hand** does the same for a well-timed Assist. Fall back on your
shortbow to pick off stragglers while your traps and orders do the heavy
lifting.

Dark Vizier
-----------
**Feint** before you strike, redirecting your next melee attack to use
Intrigue instead of Skirmish - useful when your cunning outstrips your
swordsmanship. Imbue your blade with **Geist Strike** to leave a hit target
seeing you as Invisible, or vanish outright with **Umbral Shroud**.

**Tactician** rewards you with bonus momentum for Pinpointing a target and
opens up the **Direct** Major Action, while **Helping Hand** does the same
for a well-placed Assist - letting your allies strike harder and more often
than they could alone. Keep a throwing knife ready for anyone who closes the
distance before you're ready for them.
